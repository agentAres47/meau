Delete your account

Last updated 23 July 2026

You can delete your MeAau account and personal data at any time. Here's how, and what happens.

How to request deletion

In the app: open Profile → Delete account and confirm.

By email: write to meaau@meaau.in from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my account". We may confirm your identity before proceeding.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and Amizone-verified details (name, email, department, photo).
  • Your ride history, requests, matches, and ratings.
  • Driver details, including your vehicle info and licence image, if any.
  • Your push-notification token and device data.

(As a reminder, we never stored your Amizone password in the first place.)

Timeline

Deletion is processed within 30 days of your request. A small amount of information may be kept longer only where the law requires it (for example, records needed to resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation).

This is permanent

Once deleted, your account and data cannot be recovered. If you want to use MeAau again later, you'll need to sign up and verify through Amizone afresh.
