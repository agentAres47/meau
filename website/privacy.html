Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 July 2026

MeAau is a carpool app exclusively for verified students and faculty of Amity University, Mumbai. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you have over it. Questions: meaau@meaau.in.

Who we are

MeAau ("we", "us") operates the MeAau mobile app. We are the data controller for the information described here.

What we collect

  • Amizone verification data. To confirm you belong to Amity Mumbai, we verify you once through Amizone and read your name, enrolment/email, department, and profile photo. We never store your Amizone password — it is used once to sign in, then discarded. Only a "verified" flag and the profile fields above are kept.
  • Account details. Email, and optionally phone number and profile photo.
  • Location you enter. The pickup, drop, and route for rides you post or request, plus your device's approximate location to centre the map and find rides near you. We do not track your location continuously in the background.
  • Ride activity. Rides posted and requested, matches, fares, ride history, and ratings you give or receive.
  • Driver details (only if you offer rides). Vehicle information and a photo of your driving licence, used to verify you as a driver.
  • Device & technical data. A push-notification token, basic device/app information, and error logs.

How we use it

  • Confirm you are eligible to use MeAau (Amity Mumbai only).
  • Match riders, show rides on a map, and coordinate pickups.
  • Send ride-related notifications (requests, matches, cancellations).
  • Verify drivers and support safety and trust between users.
  • Provide support and improve the app.

What we share

When you match with someone, the details needed to complete the ride — your name, photo, pickup/drop, and (for drivers) vehicle info — are shown to the other rider. We use trusted service providers to run the app: Supabase (database & storage), Google Maps (maps & routing), Expo (push notifications), and Railway (backend hosting). We do not sell your personal data and do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and associated data at any time — see Delete your account. Some records may be retained where the law requires.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your data, and withdraw consent. Contact meaau@meaau.in and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Eligibility

MeAau is for verified members of Amity University, Mumbai, aged 18 or older. It is not intended for anyone else.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
